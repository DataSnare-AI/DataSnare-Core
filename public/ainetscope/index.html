Metric help: definitions and unavailable values

Unavailable is not zero, healthy, or an application failure. It means the current evidence cannot support that metric. Analytics is a snapshot of all filtered observations; reopen it after changing filters or re-analyzing clock settings.

MetricDefinitionWhy unavailable or limited
Samples / mean / p95 / p99Count, average and interpolated percentiles of matched capture intervals in milliseconds, separated by direction.No matched samples in that direction under current filters. Without both local IPs, use Unknown direction / B minus A. Small sample tail percentiles are descriptive only.
JitterPopulation standard deviation of the matched intervals, in milliseconds.No eligible timing samples. A single sample gives zero but does not establish stable path behavior.
Negative intervalsNumber of timing samples below zero; retained in statistics.Check residual clock offset/drift, endpoint identity and capture location. Negative is not physical negative transit.
A-only / B-only / ambiguousObservations with no unique counterpart; repeated TCP signatures are ambiguous, not diagnosed retransmissions.Filters or a matched-only session omit these observations. Capture loss, offload, NAT and incomplete coverage can also prevent matching.
Aligned overlapIntersection of both captured time ranges after B clock correction.No overlapping ranges, missing timestamps, or reduced session coverage. It does not prove both captures recorded every packet.
Sender seen / receiver missingSender-only observations inside aligned overlap, in the identified direction.Requires both endpoint IPs and full capture coverage. This is a visibility gap, not confirmed loss.
Confirmed drop locationA device or path point proven to drop traffic.Intentionally unavailable from these two captures alone; additional observation points or device evidence are required.
MSS / WScaleMaximum segment size offered in SYN; window-scale exponent (receive window multiplier 2^shift).Handshake not captured, option not advertised, or incomplete option bytes. WScale 0 is valid. Both offers are needed for negotiated scaling.
SACK / TCP timestampsWhether a captured handshake advertises these TCP options.Missing/incomplete handshake. No means a complete handshake did not advertise the option, rather than unavailable.
Option differencesChanged options across a uniquely matched complete handshake.No comparable handshake pairs; zero differences does not rule out a middlebox.
Zero windowsObserved zero receive-window advertisements, excluding SYN and RST.No usable window observations, filtered-out packets, or reduced session coverage. Zero observations is not proof that no stalls occurred.
Outstanding sequence bytes / flight samplesHandshake-anchored sequence high-water minus cumulative ACK, including SYN/FIN; peak and p95 over eligible sends.Missing SYN, sequence data or ACK context; matched-only sessions omit necessary context. Gaps/reordering can inflate estimates; not exact application payload bytes.
Maximum window / window basisLargest observed advertised window; scaled when both complete scale offers exist, otherwise raw.No TCP windows. Raw values cannot be assumed to be the negotiated receive capacity.

Start with All observations and clear search when investigating unavailable metrics. Include connection context as needed. Changing local IPs or B clock offset requires re-analysis; display UTC/local and T0 references do not change analytics.
